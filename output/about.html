About Clickbust

Clickbust rewrites misleading clickbait headlines from your favourite news and entertainment sites into clear, informative titles. Click a headline and you go straight to the original article — no redirect tricks, no clickbait.

How it works

  • Fetch — Reads RSS feeds from each site
  • Analyse — An AI model checks if the headline is clickbait (vague, withholding info, exaggerated)
  • Rewrite — Clickbait headlines get replaced with specific, honest alternatives
  • Publish — The rewritten headlines are published here as a clean, static site

Example

"John Cena's 2-Part Sci-Fi Thriller Is Far Better Than His Recent Movies"

→ "Peacemaker Season 2 Proves John Cena Can Lead A Sci-Fi Hit"

Why?

If you already know the show, movie, or topic being hinted at, the clickbait title adds nothing — it just wastes your time. Clickbust gives you the information you need to decide if you're interested, without forcing you to click through.
