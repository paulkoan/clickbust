This week in tech and culture

26 July 2026

Apple’s quiet update to the MacBook Air with the M4 chip got the headlines. But the real story is the price drop on the M2 model. That machine is now the best value laptop you can buy for most people. It’s fast enough, light enough, and cheap enough to make the Pro line feel like overkill for anyone who isn’t editing video.

Meta showed off its Orion augmented reality glasses to a small group. The tech is impressive. Thin frames, full AR overlay, no wires. But they’re still years from a consumer launch. The demo was a promise, not a product. It’s hard to get excited about something you can’t buy.

In culture, the Barbie movie passed $1.4 billion at the global box office. That’s not just a number. It’s a signal that audiences want big, fun, original ideas. The studios have spent years chasing franchises and reboots. Barbie proved that a well-made, clever film with a clear voice can still dominate. The lesson is simple. Make something good. People will show up.

The AI conversation shifted this week. The focus moved from capability to cost. OpenAI and Google both announced price cuts on their API models. The race isn’t about who has the smartest model anymore. It’s about who can run it cheapest. That’s a good thing. Lower costs mean more people can build with this stuff.
