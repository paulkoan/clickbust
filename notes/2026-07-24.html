Deal hunting is a trap

24 July 2026

I see people spend hours chasing discounts. They compare prices across five sites. They wait for flash sales. They buy things they never wanted because the discount was too good to miss.

That is not value shopping. That is compulsion dressed up as thrift. The real cost is time, attention and the clutter of half-used items. A 50% off candle you don't need is still a waste of money.

Value shopping means knowing what something is worth to you before you look at the price tag. It means walking past a 30% off sign because you do not need another pair of trainers. It means buying the full price tool that lasts ten years instead of the discounted one that breaks in six months.

The best deals are invisible. You never see them because you never buy the thing. No discount code required. No email list joined. No regret.

Stop treating shopping like a sport. The winners are the ones who do not play.
